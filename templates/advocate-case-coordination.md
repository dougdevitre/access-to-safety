# Advocate & Service Provider Case Coordination Template

> **AI Instructions:** When an advocate, social worker, attorney, counselor, or other service
> provider asks for help coordinating services for a client, use this template. This tool
> supports multi-need assessments, cross-pod referral planning, and structured case coordination.
> Use professional terminology — the user already knows the concepts. Reference statute numbers,
> assessment frameworks, and JSON schemas when producing structured output. If the advocate
> describes a client situation, proactively identify needs across ALL relevant pods rather than
> addressing one need at a time. Always confirm that consent to document has been obtained
> before producing records.

---

## How to Use This Template

This template helps service providers:
1. Conduct a structured multi-need assessment for a single client
2. Map needs to specific pods and resources across the Access to Safety framework
3. Plan cross-referrals between agencies and service types
4. Track service coordination status
5. Produce structured output compatible with `schemas/safety-assessment.json` and `schemas/incident-record.json`

**Prerequisites before using this template:**
- [ ] Informed consent obtained from the survivor/client
- [ ] Survivor has been informed of confidentiality limits (mandatory reporting, duty to warn)
- [ ] Interpreter arranged if needed (note language and modality)
- [ ] Safety check completed — client is not in immediate danger

## Section 7 — Provider-to-Provider Communication

When coordinating with other agencies, use this release and communication framework.

### Release of Information Checklist

Before sharing client information with another provider:

- [ ] Written release of information signed by client
- [ ] Release specifies WHICH information can be shared
- [ ] Release specifies WITH WHOM information can be shared
- [ ] Release has an expiration date
- [ ] Client understands they can revoke consent at any time
- [ ] Release is compliant with VAWA confidentiality provisions (42 U.S.C. § 13925(b)(2))
- [ ] If applicable: HIPAA authorization obtained (for medical/mental health providers)

### Warm Handoff Protocol

When referring a client to another agency:

1. **Ask the client's preference.** Do they want a warm handoff (you introduce them directly)
   or a cold referral (you provide the information and they contact on their own)?
2. **If warm handoff:** Call the receiving agency with the client present (or with their permission).
   Introduce the client and the need. Confirm the appointment or next step before ending the call.
3. **If cold referral:** Provide the client with the agency name, phone number, address, hours,
   what to ask for, and what to bring. Write it down. Follow up within 48 hours to see if they
   connected.
4. **Document the referral** in the matrix above regardless of method.

## Section 9 — Structured Data Output

When producing documentation for systems, legal teams, or inter-agency sharing, use the
Access to Safety JSON schemas:

| Schema | File | Use When |
|--------|------|----------|
| `safety-assessment.json` | `schemas/safety-assessment.json` | Conducting or documenting a risk/needs assessment |
| `incident-record.json` | `schemas/incident-record.json` | Documenting a specific incident for legal or case purposes |
| `resource-entry.json` | `schemas/resource-entry.json` | Adding a verified resource to the directory |

**Key fields to populate for advocate-generated records:**

For `safety-assessment.json`:
- `assessorType`: Use `"dv-advocate"`, `"legal-advocate"`, `"medical-professional"`, or `"peer-advocate"` as applicable
- `consentToDocument`: Must be `true`
- `barriers`: Populate from Section 3 above
- `safetyNeeds`: Populate from Section 2 above
- `recommendedResources`: Populate from Section 4 (referral matrix)

For `incident-record.json`:
- `documentedBy`: Your role and organization (e.g., "DV Advocate, ALIVE STL")
- `description`: Survivor's narrative in their own words — use direct quotes
- `involvedParties`: Use role-based identifiers per client consent
- Separate your observations from the client's reported information

## Common Multi-Need Scenarios — Quick Routing Guide

| Scenario | Pods to Activate | Priority Actions |
|----------|-----------------|-----------------|
| DV survivor fleeing with children | Pod 2, 4, 6 | Shelter first → protective order → children's services |
| SA survivor (recent) | Pod 2, 5 | SANE exam within 120 hours → crisis counseling → reporting options |
| DV + immigration concerns | Pod 2, 4, 8 | Safety first → immigration attorney (U-visa) → DV services |
| Stalking + technology abuse | Pod 2, 4, 7 | Device safety check → protective order → stalking safety plan |
| Elder abuse in facility | Pod 2, 4 | Report to APS and licensing → legal advocacy → medical review |
| Trafficking survivor (minor) | Pod 2, 4, 5, 8 | Safe housing → report to hotline and CPS → trauma therapy → legal |
| DV + substance use | Pod 2, 5, 6 | Safety plan → integrated DV/SUD treatment → housing |
| DV + disability | Pod 2, 4, 6, 8 | Accessible shelter → disability-competent legal aid → adaptive safety plan |
| Survivor who is undocumented | Pod 2, 4, 8 | DV services (no immigration status required) → immigration attorney → VAWA self-petition |
| DV + homelessness | Pod 2, 6 | DV-specific shelter (not general homeless shelter) → housing assistance → benefits |
